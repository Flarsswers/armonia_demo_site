Music Editing Listening Test

Instructions

Welcome! In this listening test, you'll help us evaluate some edited music clips.

What you'll do:

  • First, you'll hear the "Source Music" — the original music clip we want to modify.
  • You'll see a "Source Prompt" — a text description of what the original music sounds like.
  • You'll see a "Target Prompt" — a description of how we want to change the music.
  • Then, you'll hear an "Edited Result" — this is a method's attempt to apply that change. Your task is to judge how well it worked.

Please use headphones for the best listening experience. There are no right or wrong answers — we value your honest opinion.

Listening Test

There are 5 different music editing tasks below. For each task, multiple methods have produced edited results. Please rate each result independently on all three questions.

Edited Result 1 Armonia-v8 (Proposed)

Source Music & Prompts

Source Music:

Source Prompt:

A recording of a grunge rock song.

Target Prompt:

A recording of a classical score

Edited Result

Question 1 (Target Accuracy): Does the instrument or timbre in this edited music actually sound like what the target description asks for?

For example: If the target asks to change something to a flute sound, does it actually sound like a flute?

Question 2 (Background Preservation): Besides the main sound we're trying to change, does the background music stay the same?

Are there any strange noises or parts that were incorrectly modified?

Question 3 (Overall Naturalness): Does the edited sound blend naturally? Does it sound like a coherent piece of music?

Does it sound like a complete song, or does it sound like parts were awkwardly pieced together?
